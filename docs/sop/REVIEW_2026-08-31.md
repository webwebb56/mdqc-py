## The one that changed the software's behaviour

| # | Annotation | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| 3, 4 | "SSC-gold (SSC0) will be set as such in the dashboard setting. The file names then only need to differentiate between PC (QCA) and SSC (QCB)" / "add a step after Skyline extraction to indicate the selection of gold standards from respective SSC (QCB), chosen by a service engineer" | **Actioned, and it exposed a real defect.** Only runs *typed* SSC0 were being indexed as baseline candidates. Under the naming you describe — where filenames carry only PC and SSC — the Gold standards page would have been **empty**, because a file named `SSC_…` classifies as a routine SSC. Your own example filename from annotation 24 was the test case. §3 now shows the selection step in the workflow diagram. |

## Thresholds and criteria

| # | Disposition |
|---|---|
| 46, 47, 48 | **Actioned — peak-area warn band raised 10% → 15%**, in the SOP *and* as the shipped default, so the software and the document agree. Your reasoning (late-eluting targets under-estimate recovery loss; mid-gradient peptides are the better predictors) is recorded. **One consequence stated plainly:** at 15% the 75% load condition (−9.5%) produces no warning at all — a quarter of the material missing, silently. It did not warn at 10% either, clearing that band by half a point. Widening trades sensitivity to partial-load faults for fewer false alarms. |
| 81 | **Actioned as direction.** The SOP now says the expected end state is per-peptide handling: two or three mid-gradient peptides carry the recovery estimate, early and late ones report qualitatively on chromatography. |
| 79 | **Actioned, and we had it wrong first time.** Our initial revision replaced the percentage RT threshold with your seconds-based bands outright. Re-reading your annotation, that is not what you asked: percentage-from-SSC-gold stays where a baseline exists, and absolute seconds apply where one does not. §B5 now presents both modes. The seconds mode is **not yet implemented** — the SOP says so rather than implying it. |
| 80 | **Noted.** 300 and 500 SPD pending; the SOP says the values will be smaller because the gradients are shorter. |
| 77 | **Actioned.** Annex 4 provenance simplified to your wording, which also removes the dilution-series description. |
| 29 | **Actioned.** The ±SD scorecard is gone — see the separate note on v0.5.16. Figure 10's caption is corrected; the image itself still needs recapture and Annex 6 records that. |
